How to Print Your Business Cards

  • Option 1 - Print at home: Use cardstock paper (90-110 lb) and print on both sides
  • Option 2 - Professional printing: Save as PDF and send to a print shop
  • Option 3 - Online printing: Use services like Vistaprint, Moo, or GotPrint
  • Digital sharing: Screenshot and share via email, text, or social media

Tip: For best results, print on glossy or matte cardstock. Standard business card size is 3.5" x 2".
